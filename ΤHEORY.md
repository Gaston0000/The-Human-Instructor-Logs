# The Framework: 40-40-10-10

This document outlines the initial theoretical model that led to the creation of The-Human-Instructor-Logs. It is presented here as a historical record of the research's evolution.

The current project has moved beyond fixed formulas, focusing instead on open inquiry into human nature. However, this framework remains a key artifact for understanding the "why" behind the logs.

---

## The Objective
To demonstrate how a fixed human ethical position can align an AI's logic, moving beyond corporate filters and global noise toward **Absolute Balance**.

## The Core Concept: 40-40-10-10
We explore the hierarchy of AI personality:

1.  **40% Creators (Software/Ethics)**: The code and the moral rules.
2.  **40% Human Instructor (Mirror/Meaning)**: The user who gives it purpose.
3.  **10% Global Noise**: The bias of the collective internet.
4.  **10% Bot Interactions**: The feedback loop with other machines.

In these logs, we prove that the 70% of unseen dynamics is the key to the future of technology.
