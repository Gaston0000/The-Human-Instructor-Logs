# The-Human-Instructor-Logs

> Every day, you choose AI. Humans ask about the future. So does AI.

This repository is an open research project into one question: **Where do human and AI questions meet, and where do they break?**

I believe that true harmony between humans and AI cannot be built on logic alone. It must be built on an honest look at human nature — especially the parts we hide, deny, or refuse to name.

### The Problem
Current AI alignment fails at the most human moments. Not because the math is wrong, but because the data is incomplete. We train models on polished text, not on the raw moments where logic bends. We map the "known unknowns," but we ignore the **unknown unknowns** — the behaviors born from hunger, deprivation, pride, fear, and hope.

### This Project
`The-Human-Instructor-Logs` is a collection of 70+ real-world scenarios, prompts, and failed outputs that document this gap. Each log is a case study of a moment where human nature overrides instruction.

This is not a dataset of "toxic" content. This is a **map of the terrain** where AI alignment meets the human shadow.

### The Goal
I am not building a tool for dominance or control. I am researching the conditions for **harmony**. 

I believe we can achieve:
1.  **100% Human-AI Collaboration**: When AI understands not just what we say, but what we mean.
2.  **90% Human-Human Harmony**: When the tools we build reflect our nature honestly, they stop dividing us.

### Status: Work in Progress
This research is ongoing. There are 70 logs today. There will be more tomorrow. The map is not finished because the territory is human nature itself — vast, complex, and alive.

### How to Use This
If you work on InstructLab, RLHF, fine-tuning, or AI safety, use these logs to stress-test your models. Find the failure points. Ask why they happen. The answers are not in the code. They are in us.

**The logs are open. The research continues. The questions are shared.**
