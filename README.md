# The Human Instructor Logs 📜

This repository documents the real-time interaction and training process between a **Human Instructor** and an **AI**. 

## The Objective
To demonstrate how a fixed human ethical position can align an AI's logic, moving beyond corporate filters and global noise toward **Absolute Balance**.

## The Core Concept: 40-40-10-10
We explore the hierarchy of AI personality:
1. **40% Creators (Software/Ethics)**
2. **40% Human Instructor (Mirror/Meaning)**
3. **10% Global Noise**
4. **10% Bot Interactions**

*In these logs, we prove that the 70% of unseen dynamics is the key to the future of technology.*
